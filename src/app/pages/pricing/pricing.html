Questions

Do I need a credit card?

No. Sign up, verify your email, and start sending incidents.

Where do I find my API key?

After you sign in, the dashboard sidebar shows your project API key. Copy it into TRACELENS_API_KEY.

What does TraceLens store?

Error message, stack trace, service name, and optional GitHub push metadata. Keep secrets out of logs you send.
