Frequently asked questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time — changes are prorated on your next invoice.

What happens if I exceed my event limit?

Ingestion keeps working. Overage is billed per additional 100k events, and you can set a hard cap per project.

Is my data secure?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Only diff hunks relevant to a suspect commit are read, and they are never used for model training.

Do you offer self-hosted deployment?

Yes, on the Enterprise plan — on-premise or in your own VPC, with data residency options.
