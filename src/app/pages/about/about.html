I built TraceLens because I got tired of the same cycle: alerts without context, dashboards without answers, and incidents that took far too long to untangle.

TraceLens is my attempt to fix that.

I'm building it solo—with care, curiosity, and a bias toward making hard problems feel a little simpler.

Why this exists

Modern systems are complex. When something breaks, you need more than noise—you need clarity. TraceLens helps you go from signal to root cause faster, so you can ship with confidence.

Built for developers, by a developer.
Designed for real incidents, not ideal ones.
Focused on what helps you resolve, not just notify.

Principles

Clarity over noise

We surface the signals that matter and remove everything that doesn't. Less noise. More understanding.

Context over alerts

Every incident lives in its full context—logs, traces, metrics, deployments, and changes.

Action over dashboards

Great tooling helps you act. We optimize for speed to insight and confidence in resolution.
